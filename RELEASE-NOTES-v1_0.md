**Can macOS 27 run on a 2019 Intel MacBook Pro?** Not today, and not without giving up the Mac's security. This release is the record.

**What's in it**
- The position paper (`POSITION-PAPER.md`, PDF attached): what we tried, what we measured, why we stopped, and the map for anyone who wants to try. Every figure is a measurement of a file; nothing we built ever booted.
- T2 recovery notes: the boot loop, the DFU restore that fixed it, and five rules for anyone experimenting on a T2 Mac.
- The Golden Bridge look: a cosmetic layer on stock, sealed Tahoe. Gold wallpaper (original art), dark mode and gold accent, a tuned Dock, a gold prompt, and an About panel that says plainly it is a label, not an OS version. Reversible with one script. Nothing needs root.
- The census: the tooling and workflow that measured how much Intel code is inside macOS 27.2. Text output only.

**What's not in it**
No Apple software. No kernel, driver or kernel-collection tooling. No patches.
